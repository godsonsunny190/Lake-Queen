Welcome to the Backwaters

An Escape Into
Alappuzha's Emerald Waters

Kerala calls itself God's Own Country, and nowhere does that feel more fitting than on the backwaters of Alappuzha.

Our premium luxury houseboats carry you through Vembanad Lake and its enchanting canals, past coconut groves and paddy fields stretching as far as the eye can see.

Your journey is designed to be unhurried, giving you time to settle in, take in the scenery and simply enjoy being away. Thoughtful service and comfortable accommodation make every hour aboard part of the experience.

Noon · Departure

Setting Sail from Alappuzha Jetty

The journey begins as the vessel eases into the open waters of Vembanad Lake before winding through narrower canals.

Afternoon · Scenic Cruise

Through Canals and Paddy Fields

Take in the views as paddy fields and coconut groves rise gently from the water's edge, passing quiet village shores.

Midday · Lunch on Board

Authentic Kerala Cuisine

An authentic Kerala lunch is served with fish curry, rice, and vegetables, prepared fresh in the boat's own kitchen.

Evening · Sunset & Dinner

Anchorage as the Sun Descends

As evening descends, the vessel comes to rest along a stretch of backwater, where dinner follows the sunset.

Morning · Return

Breakfast on Deck

Morning arrives with a final cruise back toward the jetty, breakfast served on deck as the backwaters stir gently awake.
