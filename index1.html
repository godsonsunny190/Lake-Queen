The Experience

The LakeQueen Luxury
Backwater Experience

Drift through the enchanting backwaters of Kerala, where time moves at the gentle pace of the water itself. Vast lagoons, narrow palm-fringed canals, and emerald-green paddy fields create a landscape that has captivated travelers for centuries.

Aboard your private LakeQueen houseboat, you'll experience this magical world in absolute comfort and privacy. Every moment is curated — from the aroma of traditional Kerala spices in your freshly prepared meals to the quiet rhythm of the boat gliding through the water.

Our experienced crew and onboard chef ensure every detail is attended to with genuine warmth. Whether you seek a romantic escape, a family adventure, or simply a moment of profound peace, the backwaters offer a sanctuary unlike any other.

Our Story

About LakeQueen
Houseboats

Born from a deep love for Kerala's backwaters and a vision to share their magic with the world, LakeQueen Houseboats began as a single vessel and a dream. Today, we are proud to operate one of Alleppey's most respected fleets of luxury houseboats.

Every houseboat in our fleet is thoughtfully designed and maintained to the highest standards. We blend traditional Kerala craftsmanship with modern amenities, creating floating sanctuaries that honor the past while embracing the comfort travelers expect today.

Questions & Answers

Frequently Asked
Questions

Everything you need to know about your LakeQueen backwater experience.

Our houseboats are categorized by size, bedroom count, and level of luxury. From the intimate Deluxe 1-bedroom perfect for couples, to our spacious Ultra Luxury 5+ bedroom vessels for large groups, each offers a unique experience. Higher tiers feature premium finishes, larger living spaces, enhanced sundecks, and additional amenities. All houseboats include AC bedrooms, private bathrooms, a dedicated crew, and onboard dining.

We offer a comprehensive range: 1-bedroom (Deluxe & Honeymoon), 2-bedroom (Premium & Family), 3-bedroom (Luxury & Heritage), 4-bedroom (Grand & Presidential), and 5+ bedroom (Royal & Imperial) houseboats. Each category is available in day cruise or overnight configurations. Specialized options include honeymoon packages with romantic touches and family-friendly vessels with extra safety features.

Booking is simple and secure. You can book directly through our website by selecting your preferred houseboat and travel dates, or call our 24/7 reservation team at +91 98765 43210. We'll confirm availability within minutes and send a detailed booking confirmation. A 30% advance payment secures your reservation, with the balance due upon arrival. We accept all major credit cards, UPI, and bank transfers.

Every package includes: private use of your selected houseboat, experienced captain and crew, onboard chef with all meals (breakfast, lunch, dinner, and welcome drinks), AC bedrooms with premium bedding and linens, complimentary Wi-Fi, scenic cruising through designated backwater routes, and all necessary safety equipment. Overnight packages additionally include evening tea and snacks. Optional add-ons like candlelight dinners, sightseeing tours, and Ayurvedic spa treatments can be arranged.

Yes, we offer flexible day cruise options ranging from 3-hour scenic tours to full 8-hour day packages. Day cruises are perfect for travelers with limited time who still want to experience the beauty of the backwaters. They include lunch, scenic cruising, and the same dedicated crew service. For the full immersive experience, we recommend our overnight packages which include sunset views, an evening on the water, and waking up to the tranquil beauty of the backwaters at sunrise.
